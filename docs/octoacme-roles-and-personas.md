# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Architects define the target solution design, technical standards, and implementation guardrails. They ensure the system can meet current goals while remaining maintainable and scalable.

### Responsibilities
- Define the technical architecture and design approach
- Review solution trade-offs against reliability, security, and scalability needs
- Align engineering decisions with enterprise standards and platform constraints
- Support dependency planning and technical risk assessment
- Partner with developers and project leaders on architectural decisions

### Goals
- Create systems that are maintainable, secure, and scalable
- Reduce rework caused by avoidable design decisions
- Ensure technical consistency across project workstreams

### Typical Communication
- Architecture review sessions and design discussions
- Technical standards and decision records
- Alignment meetings with project managers and engineering leads

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to translate business goals into feasible technical approaches
- Collaborates with Project Managers on scope, sequencing, and dependency tracking
- Advises Developers on architecture, patterns, and design constraints
- Provides guidance to stakeholders on trade-offs between speed, cost, and quality

---

## Compliance and Risk Officers

### Role Summary
Compliance and Risk Officers identify regulatory, process, and operational risks that may affect a project. They help teams make informed decisions while protecting the organization from avoidable exposure.

### Responsibilities
- Assess project risk exposure across legal, operational, and security domains
- Validate compliance with internal policies and external requirements
- Support escalation paths for high-impact issues and exceptions
- Recommend mitigation strategies and control measures
- Monitor risk trends and provide status updates to leadership

### Goals
- Reduce avoidable project, regulatory, and operational risk
- Increase visibility into critical issues before they escalate
- Ensure decisions are aligned with organizational standards and requirements

### Typical Communication
- Risk reviews, escalation meetings, and project check-ins
- Compliance and control assessments
- Regular updates to project managers and stakeholder groups

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Project Managers to track, escalate, and mitigate risk
- Advises Product Managers on trade-offs involving compliance constraints or customer impact
- Works with Developers to ensure solutions meet required controls and safeguards
- Provides context to stakeholders on why mitigation actions are necessary

---

## Change Management Leads

### Role Summary
Change Management Leads prepare teams and stakeholders for adoption, helping ensure transitions are clear, supported, and sustainable. They reduce confusion during process or technology change initiatives.

### Responsibilities
- Define adoption plans and stakeholder impact assessments
- Coordinate communications about changes, timelines, and expected behaviors
- Support training, enablement, and rollout readiness activities
- Identify resistance points and mitigate adoption blockers
- Measure readiness and adoption progress across impacted groups

### Goals
- Improve stakeholder readiness and confidence
- Increase adoption of new processes, tools, or workflows
- Reduce friction during transitions and rollouts

### Typical Communication
- Change plans, launch communications, and training sessions
- Stakeholder updates and readiness reviews
- Coordination with project and functional leads

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborates with Project Managers on communication plans and rollout sequencing
- Works with Product Managers to align change messaging with user or customer needs
- Supports Developers and team leads during adoption and feedback collection
- Coordinates with stakeholders to ensure business readiness before release or rollout

---

## Quality Assurance Leads

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads define quality expectations and ensure deliverables meet required standards before release. They partner with delivery teams to improve reliability, consistency, and confidence.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality standards, acceptance criteria, and validation plans
- Review test strategies and release readiness criteria
- Identify defects, trends, and quality risks for escalation
- Partner with developers to improve test coverage and defect prevention
- Provide status updates on quality health to leadership and stakeholders

### Goals
- Improve confidence in project outcomes and release quality
- Reduce defects escaping into production or customer workflows
- Maintain consistent quality standards across deliveries

### Typical Communication
- Test planning, defect reviews, and release readiness checkpoints
- Quality dashboards and status reports
- Collaboration with engineering and project management forums

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to validate functionality and support corrective action
- Reports quality trends and risks to Project Managers for mitigation planning
- Aligns with Product Managers on user experience and business quality expectations
- Supports stakeholders with evidence that work meets defined requirements

---

## Subject Matter Experts

### Role Summary
Subject Matter Experts provide domain knowledge and business context to guide decisions, validate requirements, and improve solution quality. They help teams understand the problem and evaluate trade-offs in practical terms.

### Responsibilities
- Provide expertise on business processes, workflows, and constraints
- Review requirements, proposed solutions, and validation steps for accuracy
- Clarify domain-specific expectations and edge cases
- Support training and knowledge transfer for new or complex features
- Help identify operational and user impacts of proposed changes

### Goals
- Improve solution relevance and business fit
- Reduce misunderstandings that lead to rework or poor adoption
- Support informed decision-making with practical expertise

### Typical Communication
- Working sessions with project and product teams
- Requirement reviews and validation walkthroughs
- Knowledge-sharing conversations during planning and rollout

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Advises Product Managers on customer and business context
- Supports Project Managers with stakeholder-specific insights and risk awareness
- Helps Developers understand domain constraints and expected user behavior
- Provides stakeholders with confidence that the solution reflects real-world needs

- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
