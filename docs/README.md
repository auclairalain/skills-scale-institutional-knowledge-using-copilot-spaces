# OctoAcme Project Management Process Documentation

This README serves as the central entry point for the OctoAcme project management documentation set. It provides a concise overview of how OctoAcme structures work across initiation, planning, execution, release, and continuous improvement, while also linking directly to the detailed process guides in this folder.

## Overview of OctoAcme Project Management Processes

OctoAcme follows a customer-first, iterative project model centered on clear ownership, measurable outcomes, and continuous learning. Each project begins with a clear problem statement and stakeholder alignment, then moves into planning, execution, and release activities with regular communication and visible progress tracking. The approach emphasizes delivering small, testable increments rather than waiting for large, risky launches, and it relies on evidence, metrics, and team feedback to guide decisions.

The process is organized around a five-stage lifecycle: initiation, planning, execution, release, and close/retrospective. Project managers coordinate scope, schedules, dependencies, and communication, while product managers define outcomes and prioritize work to maximize customer value. Developers and QA collaborate to build and validate the work, and stakeholders provide input and approvals as needed. The methodology also incorporates psychological safety, clear accountability, and a strong focus on quality and transparency.

## Core Process Areas

- Initiation: define the problem, align stakeholders, and validate the business case.
- Planning: break work into milestones, dependencies, and backlog items with clear ownership.
- Execution & Tracking: run the team rhythm with standups, PR review gates, project boards, and status reporting.
- Risk Management & Communication: maintain a risk register, share updates, and escalate blockers appropriately.
- Release & Deployment: verify readiness, deploy incrementally, and monitor outcomes.
- Retrospective & Continuous Improvement: capture learnings and convert them into future improvements.

## Key Roles

- Project Manager (PM): coordinates delivery, dependencies, risks, and communication.
- Product Manager (PdM): defines the roadmap, priorities, and success metrics.
- Developers: implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria.
- QA/Testing: validates quality and acceptance outcomes.
- Stakeholders: provide direction, feedback, approvals, and business context.

## Communication Cadence

OctoAcme expects regular communication to keep teams and stakeholders aligned. This includes weekly PM/Product Manager syncs, twice-weekly standups, monthly stakeholder updates, and ad-hoc escalations for major blockers or risks. A single source of truth for project status and a consistent risk register help keep information transparent, actionable, and easy to share.
